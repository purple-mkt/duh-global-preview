Korean Language Program

Build the Korean skills you need for study, work and daily life through systematic, level-based integrated instruction.

Korean Program

Regular Course Faculty & Staff Notices

Course Overview

Integrated instruction covering speaking, listening, reading and writing together

Our Korean language program uses an integrated approach that balances listening, speaking, reading and writing within a single class. A placement test before enrolment assesses your current level so that students of similar ability study together and progress smoothly. Alongside TOPIK preparation, you will build practical Korean you can use right away for degree study and daily life in Korea.

Regular Course

Starting Fall 2026 (September) · A semester-based regular course progressing from Level 1 to Level 4

Each semester runs about 10 weeks, with 160–180 hours per level. Once you meet the goals of a level you move up, completing Levels 1 through 4 in sequence.

LevelLearning goalsHours
Level 1Learn the Hangul alphabet and basic pronunciation, and start everyday conversation with basics such as introductions and greetings.160 hours
Level 2Use expressions needed in everyday situations such as shopping, making plans and asking directions to form and understand simple sentences.160 hours
Level 3Express your views on familiar social topics, read and write short texts, and prepare for intermediate TOPIK.180 hours
Level 4Use the Korean needed for degree study and the workplace, with logical writing and presentation skills.180 hours
