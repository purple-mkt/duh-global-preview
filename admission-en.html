Admissions (Korean Language Program)

Eligibility, registration steps, required documents and visa guidance for the Korean language program (D-4).

Admissions

Eligibility Required Documents Notes

Eligibility

International applicants who wish to learn Korean may apply to the Korean language program.
  • Applicants whose own and both parents’ nationalities are non-Korean
  • Applicants who have graduated (or expect to graduate) from high school in Korea or abroad, or hold an equivalent or higher qualification
  • Applicants motivated to learn Korean (beginners welcome; no TOPIK level required)
  • Applicants able to cover the costs of study and residence in Korea

Application Process

From consultation to registration in five steps.
Consultation
Contact the Office of International Affairs
Apply
Apply online or in person and submit documents
Document Review
Eligibility and documents are reviewed
Result
Notified individually
Registration & Visa
Pay tuition and receive the Certificate of Admission

Notes

Academic document verification you must check before applying.

Academic documents issued abroad must go through the verification procedure set for each country in order to be accepted, to prevent forgery. Verification can take several weeks, so please prepare well ahead of the application deadline.

CategoryVerification method
Chinese nationalsSubmit a CHSI verification report for your academic record and degree
Non-Chinese nationalsApostille certification, or consular verification by the Korean consulate in your country
Verification procedures and accepted qualifications vary by country and may change. Some qualifications, such as vocational high school diplomas, require separate confirmation, so please contact the Office of International Affairs before applying.
