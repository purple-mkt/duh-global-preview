Leisure Landscape

The Office of International Affairs recruits international students into Leisure Landscape and Smart Food Resources two majors. Field-based education develops skilled professionals for the leisure and landscape industry.

Majors

Leisure LandscapeSmart Food Resources

About the Major

We train hands-on specialists through landscape fundamentals and field-based education.

The Leisure Landscape major builds on landscape fundamentals and field-based learning to develop skilled professionals for the leisure and landscape industry. Grounded in character and expertise, graduates are creative problem-solvers who combine Fourth Industrial Revolution technology with ecological value and cultural sensibility to lead a sustainable future for cities and the environment.

Smart drone surveying, landscape capstone design, garden design studio and more Field- and practice-centred curriculum, supporting a range of certifications across landscaping, horticulture and drone operation.

Certifications Available

We support students in earning certifications in landscaping, horticulture and drone operation.
  • Landscaping Landscape Craftsman · Landscape Industrial Engineer · Natural Ecosystem Restoration Industrial Engineer
  • Horticulture & Plants Horticulture Craftsman · Protected Horticulture Industrial Engineer · Plant Protection Industrial Engineer · Organic Farming Industrial Engineer
  • Garden & Golf Greenkeeper · Golf course manager · Garden-related certifications
  • Drone Drone certifications (Classes 1–4)
