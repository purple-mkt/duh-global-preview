Smart Food Resources

The Office of International Affairs recruits international students into Leisure Landscape and Smart Food Resources two majors. We train specialists in smart farming and the insect industry to meet the challenges of global climate change.

Majors

Leisure LandscapeSmart Food Resources

About the Major

A future-focused major leading smart farming and the insect industry.

The Smart Food Resources major trains specialists in smart food resources (indoor and open-field smart farms) to meet global climate change, as well as professionals for the fast-growing smart farm and insect industries. Intensive hands-on education centres on our insect-industry smart farm factory laboratory, producing NCS work-ready graduates in partnership with the Yeongam County education district and its leased smart farm development projects.

Established in 2024. Designated in March 2024 as a special-field in-service training institution for primary and secondary teachers, the major also runs an Insect Consultant certification course for adult learners.

Certifications Available

We support students in earning national and private certifications.
  • National Sanitarian (licence) · Care Farming Practitioner (certification)
  • Private Insect Consultant · Insect Interpreter · Edible Insect Cook · Healthy Food Master (edible insect cuisine)
