About the Office

Supporting international students in learning Korean and living in Korea.

For international students who wish to learn Korean, the Office of International Affairs at Donga University of Health centres its work on running the Korean language program, supporting every part of student life from admissions and registration to visa, insurance, dormitory and settling in. Students from Vietnam and many other countries learn Korean language and culture on campus as they prepare for their next step.
